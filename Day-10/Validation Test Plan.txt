Validation Test Plan – GenAI Resume Screening Tool
Use Case

The proposed GenAI tool screens incoming resumes and recommends either:

Advance – The candidate should move forward in the recruitment process.
Reject – The candidate should not move forward.

Before deploying the tool, it is important to validate that its recommendations are accurate, fair, and reliable across different types of resumes.

1. Validate Recommendation Accuracy

The first step is to check whether the tool is making appropriate Advance or Reject recommendations.

The AI's recommendations should be compared with decisions made by experienced human recruiters using a representative set of resumes.

Edge Case

A highly qualified candidate may use different terminology to describe their skills than the terminology expected by the model.

For example, the resume may describe relevant experience using different wording but still meet the job requirements. The tool should not reject a strong candidate simply because the exact keywords are missing.

2. Validate Fairness and Consistency

The tool should be tested to ensure that recommendations are based on relevant qualifications, skills, and experience rather than irrelevant personal information.

Candidates with similar qualifications should receive consistent recommendations.

Edge Case

Two resumes contain the same skills and experience, but details such as the candidate's name are different.

The recommendation should remain consistent and should not change because of irrelevant information.

3. Validate Performance with Different Resume Types

The tool should be tested using different resume formats and real-world scenarios.

This includes resumes with different structures, incomplete information, employment gaps, or unusual formatting.

Edge Case

A candidate may have a career gap or a resume with some missing information.

The system should evaluate the available qualifications properly rather than automatically rejecting the candidate because the resume does not follow a standard format.

Who Should Validate the Tool?

The team that developed the tool should not be the only team responsible for validation.

An independent validation team, along with experienced recruitment or HR professionals, should participate in the testing process.

This is important because the development team may already have assumptions about how the system should behave. Independent reviewers can provide a more objective evaluation and may identify issues that the development team missed.

Summary

Before the GenAI resume screening tool goes live, the validation process should focus on:

Accuracy – Are the Advance and Reject recommendations appropriate?
Fairness – Are similar candidates treated consistently?
Robustness – Can the system handle different and unusual resume formats?

The validation should also be performed independently to ensure that the results are objective and trustworthy.
